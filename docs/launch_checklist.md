# Launch Checklist

| Timing | Owner | Check |
|---|---|---|
| T-6 weeks | Partnerships / Legal | Contract terms, data-sharing purpose, consent language, reward liability. |
| T-4 weeks | Marketing | English and Traditional Chinese CRM, partner landing-page copy, approval record. |
| T-3 weeks | Product / Operations | Airport zones, reward eligibility, expiry, cancellation, and refund rules. |
| T-2 weeks | Analytics | Cohort query peer review, Control holdout, dashboard refresh, data-quality alerts. |
| T-1 week | Marketplace | Driver availability forecast, peak-hour tactic, support macro, escalation channel. |
| T-1 day | All teams | Dry-run one test booking, promotion, cancellation, refund, and dashboard receipt. |
| Launch | Operations | Hourly service-quality review and partner issue log. |
| Weekly | Partnerships | Business review: spend, conversion, repeat, ETA, cancellations, and next action. |
| Close | Analytics / Operations | Incrementality readout, budget reconciliation, and postmortem. |
