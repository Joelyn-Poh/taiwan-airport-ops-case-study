# Campaign Brief: TPE Arrival to Local

## Goal

Convert eligible arriving travelers into an airport trip and a subsequent local trip while holding marketplace service quality within guardrails.

## Scope

- Period: 2025-09-01 to 2025-10-26.
- Partner: fictional travel platform `Travel Partner X`.
- Channel: partner referral plus CRM for opted-in users.
- Location: Taoyuan International Airport to Taipei / Taoyuan service zones.

## Eligibility

1. Has an eligible Partner X arrival booking during the campaign.
2. Has consented to CRM when the message is sent.
3. Has not completed a Taiwan ride in the prior 180 days.
4. Is not on fraud, duplicated-account, or prior-promotion exclusion lists.
5. Is assigned before the booked arrival, and receives the CRM send between assignment and arrival.

## Experiment

| Arm | Allocation | Reward |
|---|---:|---|
| Control | 10% | Information / partner landing page only |
| Airport 150 | 45% | NT$150 off one airport trip |
| Bundle 100+100 | 45% | NT$100 off airport trip plus NT$100 off a second trip within 7 days |

## Decision gate

Roll out a scheme only when its incremental contribution is positive, the 95% confidence interval of incremental conversion excludes zero, P90 pickup ETA increases by no more than 10%, and cancellation increases by no more than 1 percentage point.
